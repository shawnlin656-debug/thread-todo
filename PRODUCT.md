# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is an individual working on a Mac who needs a lightweight task companion that can stay at the edge of the screen throughout the workday. They frequently receive interruptions and need to capture urgent work without losing their previous context.

## Product Purpose

Thread preserves a user's task context across interruptions. It allows a temporary task to take focus immediately, keeps the interrupted task visible and recoverable, and guides the user back when the interruption is complete. Success means fewer forgotten tasks and less effort spent reprioritizing after every interruption.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, reassuring. The product should feel native to macOS: quiet when not needed, clear at the moment of interruption, and trustworthy about preserving the user's place.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page composition, oversized editorial typography, warm paper textures, neon accents, ornamental dashboards, and decorative glass cards. It should not feel like a website pretending to be an app.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the thread: every interruption must have an obvious and reliable return path.
2. One focus at a time: the current task is visually dominant without hiding what comes next.
3. Stay at the edge: the interface must remain useful in a narrow screen-side footprint.
4. Reveal detail only when needed: default to a quiet, low-cognitive-load surface.
5. Use familiar macOS behaviors so the tool disappears into the user's workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow the operating system's light or dark appearance automatically. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Maintain readable contrast in both appearances, never encode priority through color alone, and expose clear labels and keyboard-accessible controls.
